Steps 1 and 2 each apply one patch, which `apply.py` stages; step 3 runs the old-pin leg over step 2's commit, and step
4 the sweep, then the record. **Each step is one commit, pushed**: its block, then its entry, then `git commit` of

log first; a different emission digest is a compiler defect to raise, not a pin to move, and a refused asset is a
finding to report, never a digest to change.
