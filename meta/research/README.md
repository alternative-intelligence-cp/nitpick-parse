# `meta/research/`

Format specification notes, corpus provenance, and any measurement a decision
cites. A decision that cites a measurement keeps the measurement here.

[`CURRENCY.md`](CURRENCY.md) is the table of every outside fact the plan rests on, with the day each
was checked (PA-112); a research digest is filed beside it as `<topic>.md` and cited by the row
it checks.
